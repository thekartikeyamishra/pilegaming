# Privacy Policy for Pile

**Last Updated: July 7, 2026**

Kartikeya Mishra ("we," "our," or "us") built the Pile app as a Free/Commercial app. This service is provided by us and is intended for use as is.

Because Pile is built with an offline-first philosophy, we value your privacy above all else. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled within the app.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**Pile does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.** 

* **No Accounts:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use the app.
* **No Cloud Storage / Servers:** All data regarding your video game collection, tracking history, budgets, and prices paid stays completely on your local device. We do not run any backend servers to store your library.
* **No Third-Party Analytics:** We do not track your behavior, clicks, or crashes using analytics software.

## 2. Permissions & Features

* **Local Notifications:** The app requests permission to schedule local notifications on your device purely to countdown game release days. No data leaves your device for this.
* **Sharing Features:** When you use the "Shame Card" feature, a screenshot is generated entirely locally on your device. The image and text are only shared externally when you explicitly tap the native share button.
* **External Links (Deals Toolbox):** The app contains links to external authorized storefronts. Opening these links opens your standard mobile browser. Pile does not transmit any user identifier or personal data to these partners.

## 3. Data Deletion

Because your data lives exclusively in your device's local storage (via shared preferences), **deleting the Pile app will permanently erase all of your library data.** We do not keep any backup copy because we do not have access to your data.

## 4. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. 

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
